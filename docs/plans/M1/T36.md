## Details

**T36 Consent outreach (user, day 1).** Contact 5–6 prospects, ideally including a WordPress + Elementor site and a WordPress + Divi site to fill manifest slots 14–15 (ADR-004). The request should say plainly: we'll scan up to 25 pages of your site, take screenshots and run speed and accessibility tools; the results are private to you; we delete scan data after 90 days unless you hire us (D18). It should also ask, separately, whether we may keep the site as an anonymized test case (D17). Record each yes in T29, and in the manifest as `granted` only if they agreed to the test-site use.
