## Details

**T18 HTTPS, H1, dead links, contrast.**
- `https`: the final URL is https, and the `http://` start URL redirects to https (seen in the redirect chain during the crawl, not by a separate probe). Lighthouse `is-on-https` supplies mixed-content evidence.
- `h1-present`: from axe `page-has-heading-one`, plus our own `headings` check that the H1 is visible.
- `dead-links` (D7):
  - `href="#"` or `javascript:` on a link that is not a toggle.
  - A same-page `#id` with no target.
  - Hosts on a defunct-services list (`plus.google.com`, `google.com/+`, `vine.co`).
  - Same-origin links whose crawl status was ≥ 400, plus HEAD checks of uncrawled same-origin links through `guardedFetch`: at most 50, robots respected, not counted as pages.
  - External links are never requested.
- `text-contrast` (D14): axe `color-contrast` (4.5:1 normal text, 3:1 large), grouped per color pair, with the measured ratio as evidence.
*Acceptance:* section 5 rows; chambers' `#contact` is silent; iphci's Services toggle is silent; hair-and-co's Google+ fires.
