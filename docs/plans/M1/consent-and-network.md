# M1 section 4. Consent, fixtures and network policy

Part of the [M1 plan](../M1.md).

- **Who may be crawled:** chambers-consulting.dev (own-site) and prospects with consent recorded in T29. No manifest site with `consent: none` is crawled, fetched, HEAD-checked or run through Lighthouse. That includes the `https` check: we never probe `http://` on a site we don't have consent for.
- **Two consent records, two purposes.** `project.consent` (DB) allows the audit. A manifest entry becomes `granted` only if the owner also agreed to the test-site use (D17). `selectNetworkEligible()` stays the only gate for evals and fixture recording.
- **Offline tests and evals.** Every capture test runs against `evals/fixtures` on loopback with external requests aborted (T4). CI never touches the internet.
- **Fixture sources:**
  1. Anonymized label-derived synthetic sites (T5–T6). Structure and patterns come from the labels; names, numbers, emails, addresses and people are fictional.
  2. Pattern fixtures with no label behind them.
  3. Real captures of network-eligible sites only (T34, later consenting prospects).
- **Limitation, stated plainly:** synthetic sites are generated by us, so they test our handling of patterns we already know about, not unknown real-world mess. Real-world accuracy is measured only on chambers-consulting and on prospects who agree to the test-site use.
- **Prospect data:** private buckets, signed URLs, deleted after 90 days for prospects who don't engage (D18), deleted on request at any time.
