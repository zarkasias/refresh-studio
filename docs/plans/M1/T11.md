## Details

**T11 Style facts and font map.** `fonts/normalize.ts` is a pure function with a lookup table. The table covers the Wix internal names seen in the set (`avenir-lt-w01_35-light1475496` → Avenir, `din-next-w01-light` → DIN Next, Futura, and common Wix families) plus Squarespace/Typekit aliases. Fallback: strip `-w01…`, `-lt-`, trailing `_\d+`, numeric suffixes and weight words, then title-case. A system stack becomes `system-ui`. Colors: computed `color` and `background-color` per visible text node, weighted by text area, then mapped to lowercase hex. *Acceptance:* table-driven tests; casper and arc fixtures give exactly the families in their labels (`Futura, Cormorant Garamond, Bitter, Georgia`; `Avenir, DIN Next, Open Sans`), and each has `raw` set.
