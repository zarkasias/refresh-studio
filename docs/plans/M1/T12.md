## Details

**T12 Platform detection.** `detectPlatform(signals: PlatformSignals[]): { platform, platformDetails, evidence, confidence }` is pure and rule-table driven (`platform/rules.ts`). Every rule emits `{ signal, where }` evidence in the site-model shape.
- **wix:** `static.wixstatic.com`/`parastorage.com` asset hosts, generator "Wix.com Website Builder", `x-wix-request-id` header, `*.wixsite.com` host, footer "created with Wix.com".
- **squarespace:** `squarespace-cdn.com`/`static1.squarespace.com`, `Static.SQUARESPACE_CONTEXT`, the `This is Squarespace.` comment.
- **wordpress:** `/wp-content/`, `/wp-includes/`, `<link rel="https://api.w.org/">`, generator WordPress, jQuery Migrate. Page builders:
  - **elementor:** `elementor-` classes, `/plugins/elementor/`, generator Elementor.
  - **divi:** `et_pb_` classes, `/themes/Divi/`, `et_divi_theme` body class.
  - **wpbakery:** `js_composer`, `vc_row`/`wpb_` classes.
  - **beaver-builder:** `fl-builder` body class, `fl-row`, `/plugins/bb-plugin/` or `/uploads/bb-plugin/`.
  - **kadence-blocks:** `wp-block-kadence-*`, `/plugins/kadence-blocks/`.
  - **gutenberg:** `wp-block-` classes only when no other builder matched.
  - `theme` comes from `/themes/<name>/` and `wp-theme-<name>`, kept as found (`kadence`).
- **custom:** no CMS signals, with framework evidence (`/_next/`, `__NEXT_DATA__`, Astro).
- Also webflow, godaddy, and other-hosted with `builder` from footer text, one rule each.
- Confidence: two or more independent strong signals → 0.9; one → 0.75; custom by absence → 0.6; nothing → `unknown`, 0.
*Acceptance:* all 13 manifest entries give the manifest platform and pageBuilder/builder from signals built from their recorded evidence, except where D13 says otherwise, plus the four pattern builders. When two builders appear, the one with more layout nodes wins and both appear in evidence.
