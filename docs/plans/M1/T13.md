## Details

**T13 Site assembly and CLI.** `assembleSite(run, pages): Site` (v3) holds platform (T12), brand (fonts/colors, T11) and business. Business fields: phones (displayed text, first = most frequent in header and contact), emails, address (JSON-LD, else US street+ZIP pattern in footer/contact), hours entries labeled by region/page (`"Footer"`, `"/contact"`), socialLinks (known host list → slug), googleBusinessUrl (`g.page`, `maps.app.goo.gl`, `google.com/maps`). Pages get path, title and meta; **sections: [] until the M2 mapper**. Validated with `SiteSchema`. CLI: `pnpm --filter capture crawl --project <id>` (DB) and `--fixture <slug>` (T4 server). It refuses a bare URL with no project. *Acceptance:* `pnpm eval:capture` compares each fixture's assembled Site business/brand/platform block with the anonymized label (scored in T25) and its facts with `expected.capture.json` (exact).
