## Details

**T17 Hours and NAP checks.** `hours/parse.ts` is a small deterministic parser. It reads day names, abbreviations and ranges (`Mon–Fri`, `Monday to THURSDAY`, `Friday & SATURDAY`, `MONDAY | 9AM-8PM`) and times (`7:30a`, `8am`, `07:30 AM`, `9AM–5PM`, `Closed`, `24/7`) into `{ day → [open, close] | closed }`. Anything it can't read is `unparsed` and never fires.
- `hours-consistent` (D12): compare entries of the same scope. Entries whose label or nearby heading says emergency, kitchen, brunch, holiday, happy hour, `24/7`, or a location label are separate scopes. Fails when the same day has different times in two entries. A day missing from one entry is not a conflict.
- `hours-visible`, `nap-consistent` (name variants after normalizing `&`/"and"/punctuation; phone digit sets per location; location count per page), `local-business-schema` (JSON-LD `@type` is LocalBusiness or a schema.org subtype; `name` and `telephone` or `address` present).
*Acceptance:* section 5 rows; parser table tests cover every hours string in the 10 labels.
