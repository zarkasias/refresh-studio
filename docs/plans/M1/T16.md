## Details

**T16 Phone and contact checks** (pure, over `CapturedPage[]` plus `Site`):
- `tel-matches-display`: percent-decode the href, strip invisible characters, keep digits, drop a leading NANP `1`. Compare with the digits in the link text. If the link text has no number, compare with the site's displayed numbers. Fails when they differ.
- `tel-hidden-characters`: the raw href contains encoded or literal U+200B–U+200D, U+2060, U+FEFF, U+00AD, U+200E/F or U+202A–E. Encoded `( ) space -` are fine.
- `tap-to-call`: a visible `phoneTexts` entry with `insideTelLink: false`, one finding per page and region.
- `contact-above-fold-mobile`: a call/book/contact link with `box.y + h ≤ 844` at 390.
- `form-works`: every field has a label, and the form has a submit control.
- `google-business-link`.
*Acceptance:* the section 5 matrix rows for these checks pass on fixtures, including every negative case.
